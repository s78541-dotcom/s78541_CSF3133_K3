Overview & History

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in 1979, providing facilities for students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programs to conduct practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the centre was upgraded to a branch campus in 1996 before being granted autonomy as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, it was officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, solidifying its niche focus on marine science, aquatic resources, and environmental sustainability.

Today, UMT stands as a premier institution for oceanography and marine research in Southeast Asia, attracting researchers and students globally to study coastal ecosystems, maritime management, and sustainable biodiversity.
